What gets filled: Opening Liquor Weights, Opening Wine Count, and Opening Bottles & Cans counts (from the Opening Counts panel above, whenever you save it), Close Liquor Weights, Wine Changeover, the Requisitions log (one line per pull, matching how the sheet already tracks it), Bottles & Cans Changeover counts, the Draught Sprint Meter Pour Oz. column (matched tap-by-tap to the right brand), and — from the Pixel report — Pixel Rung In, Draught, and Bottles & Cans sold quantities.
What's never touched: every formula, the BAR sheet, cell colours, and anything you don't explicitly fill in. Open weights are only ever written when you type a number into the Opening Counts panel above — photos and the sales report never touch them.
Not yet supported: individual per-bottle weigh-in backup sheets — those still need to be entered by hand.
Default template: drop your blank FD LIQ WEIGHTS workbook once and it's remembered in this browser automatically — no button to click, no more dropping it in nightly. To update it (new year, a formula fix, etc.), just drop the new version and it silently replaces the old one. Running a fill never touches the saved template — only dropping a new .xlsx does.
